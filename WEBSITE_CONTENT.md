## 4. HOW IT WORKS PAGE

### Headline
"From Measurement to Perfection: The Satvik Savor Process"

---

### Sensor Technology Section

**Salt Detection (Electrochemical Sensor)**
How it works: "Sodium ions in food create electrical potential differences. Our sensor measures this voltage and calculates exact salt concentration (in mg/100ml)."
- Target range: 0.5-2.0% sodium
- Measurement method: Ion-selective electrode
- Calibration: Monthly
- Accuracy: ±2%

**Sweetness Measurement (Optical Sensor)**
How it works: "Different concentrations of sugars bend light differently. Our optical sensor measures light refraction to calculate sweetness levels (in Brix units)."
- Target range: 2-15 Brix
- Measurement method: Refractive index
- Calibration: Per batch
- Accuracy: ±0.5 Brix

**Sourness Detection (pH Sensor)**
How it works: "Acidity creates hydrogen ions that our pH sensor detects, measuring sourness on the pH scale (0-14)."
- Target range: pH 3.5-6.5
- Measurement method: pH electrode
- Calibration: Daily
- Accuracy: ±0.1 pH

**Spice Level Measurement (Chemical Sensor)**
How it works: "Capsaicin and other spice compounds trigger our chemical sensor, quantifying heat in Scoville units."
- Target range: 0-50,000 Scoville Units
- Measurement method: Electrochemical detection
- Calibration: Per recipe
- Accuracy: ±5%

**Temperature Monitoring (Thermal Sensor)**
How it works: "Precise thermistor measures food temperature in real-time, ensuring optimal cooking temperature."
- Range: 0°C to 100°C
- Measurement method: Thermistor
- Accuracy: ±1°C

---

### Data Processing Pipeline Section

**Step 1: Signal Acquisition**
- Raw sensor signals collected from 5 sensors
- Sampling rate: 100 Hz (100 readings per second)
- Duration: 5 seconds continuous measurement

**Step 2: Signal Filtering**
- Remove noise and electrical interference
- Apply moving average filter
- Calibrate against baseline values
- Normalize data (0-100 scale)

**Step 3: Feature Extraction**
- Calculate mean, median, standard deviation
- Detect anomalies in readings
- Compare against recipe baseline
- Generate consistency score

**Step 4: Cloud Processing**
- Upload to secure cloud servers
- Apply machine learning models
- Generate recommendations
- Cross-reference with historical data

**Step 5: Real-Time Feedback**
- Display results on mobile app
- Send push notifications
- Trigger alerts if out of range
- Log data for future analysis

---

### Integration Steps Section

**Step 1: Device Setup (5 minutes)**
- Unbox and charge device
- Download mobile app
- Connect to WiFi
- Pair with phone via Bluetooth

**Step 2: Recipe Creation (10 minutes)**
- Create new recipe in app
